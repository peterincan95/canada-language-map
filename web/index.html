Canada's Linguistic Tapestry

Respondents to the 2021 Canadian Census reported speaking over 200 languages at home across the country. Use this map to explore what Canadians are speaking and where.

The Official Languages view breaks down speakers of Canada's two official languages English and French, and all other languages. The Other Languages view breaks down the speakers of all 200+ non-official languages.
